🚀 HOW TO USE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Start the app:
   $ python src/app.py

2. Open Knowledge Base menu:
   > kb

3. Select "2. Add document"

4. Choose a chunking strategy:
   - Paragraphs: General documents
   - Sentences: Technical docs
   - Size-based: Uniform processing
   - Sliding Window: Books & tutorials
   - Semantic: Mixed-topic documents

5. Watch the magic:
   Document is parsed and chunked intelligently!

📈 PERFORMANCE CHARACTERISTICS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Sliding Window:
  - Most chunks (context overlap)
  - Best for: Continuous narrative
  - Overlap: 50%

Semantic:
  - Fewest chunks (topic-aware)
  - Best for: Mixed-topic content
  - Uses NLTK sentence tokenization

Size-based:
  - Most uniform distribution
  - Best for: Predictable processing
  - No content awareness

Sentences:
  - Moderate chunks
  - Best for: Technical content
  - Fact-based organization

Paragraphs:
  - Respects natural boundaries
  - Best for: Essays and articles
  - Preserves author's structure
